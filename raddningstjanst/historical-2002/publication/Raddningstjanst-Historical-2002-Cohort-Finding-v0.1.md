# Räddningstjänst — Historical 2002 Cohort
## Finding v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Status:** FROZEN RESULT  
**Design status:** Prespecified estimator; post-unmask data-availability amendment frozen before estimation  
**Outcome regime:** Historical Insatsrapport-96 / arrival-extent regime  
**Primary analysis years:** 1998–2000 vs 2003–2004  
**Treatment year:** 2002  
**Anticipation / implementation excluded:** 2001–2002

## 1. Research question

Among Swedish municipalities entering their first verified full pooled rescue-service authority arrangement in 2002, did the population rate of building fires that had spread beyond the start object by the time the rescue service arrived change differently from structurally eligible municipalities that had not yet entered full pooled authority?

The study estimates a cohort-level short-run association/effect signal for **entry into full authority pooling (`A`)**, not a universal effect of municipal mergers, rescue-service federations, or common boards.

The treated cohort contains **5 independent institutional events / 9 municipalities**. The frozen structural control risk set contains **20 future-treatment institutional events / 33 municipalities**. The institutional event/system, not the municipality, is the unit of causal independence.

## 2. Frozen primary outcome

Primary outcome: `ARRIVAL_BEYOND_START_OBJECT_RATE_PER_100K`.

Primary numerator:
- `I startutrymmet`
- `I flera rum`
- `I flera brandceller`

Exposure: `M_t=(P_{t-1}+P_t)/2` using annual mean population.

The primary outcome captures fire spread **already present when the rescue service arrives**. It is not a direct measure of final damage or suppression performance after arrival.

## 3. Frozen estimator

`Y_et ~ Poisson(mu_et)`

`log(mu_et) = alpha_e + gamma_t + beta*(Treated_e × Post_t) + log(Exposure_et)`

where `alpha_e` are institutional-event fixed effects, `gamma_t` unrestricted calendar-year fixed effects, `Post` = 2003–2004, and `exp(beta)` is the primary incidence-rate ratio.

Interpretation was frozen before post-outcome unmasking:
- `IRR < 1` = treated-favorable relative change
- `IRR > 1` = treated-adverse relative change

No binary `p < .05` success/failure rule is used.

## 4. Post-unmask data-availability amendment

MCF confirmed complete incident-report absence for Ekerö 2003, Ekerö 2004 and Bjuv 2004. These municipality-years were classified as `STRUCTURAL_REPORTING_ABSENCE`.

They were never coded as zero, and their population exposure was not retained in the denominator. No replacement control municipalities were introduced.

The frozen amendment specified (1) available-observation primary execution and (2) a complete-control-event sensitivity excluding `EV_SBFF_2007` and `EV_RSNV_BJUV_2019` from all years.

All 18 treated post-period municipality-years are observed.

## 5. Primary result

The locked primary estimate is:

**IRR = 1.9530**

This corresponds to an approximately **95% larger relative change** in the primary arrival-spread rate among treated systems than among controls.

Uncertainty intervals:
- Pearson-dispersion-scaled model interval: **1.2165–3.1355**
- event-cluster sandwich interval: **1.1620–3.2827**

Both intervals remain above 1. This is a strong adverse directional signal within the frozen cohort. It is not, by itself, a universal causal estimate for full authority pooling.

## 6. Robustness

### Pre-window
- Primary: **1.953**
- endpoints-only pre (`1998 + 2000`): **1.806**
- latest-clean-pre (`2000`): **1.729**

Classification: `PREWINDOW_DIRECTION_ROBUST`.

### Item nonresponse
Frozen adversarial `Ej angiven` bounds: **1.534–2.018**.

Classification: `MISSINGNESS_DIRECTION_ROBUST`.

### Structural reporting absence
Complete-control-event sensitivity: **IRR 1.948**.

Classification: `DATA_AVAILABILITY_DIRECTION_ROBUST`.

### Leave-one-treated-event-out
Five refits: **1.811–2.039**. All remain above 1.

Classification: `TREATED_EVENT_DIRECTION_ROBUST`.

### Leave-one-control-event-out
Twenty refits: **1.486–2.146**. All remain above 1.

Classification: `CONTROL_EVENT_DIRECTION_ROBUST`.

The most influential control system is `EV_RSYD_2006_FIRST`; removing it reduces the estimate to approximately 1.49 but does not reverse direction.

## 7. Event-level pattern

All five treated institutional events show a raw pre/post rate ratio above 1:
- Falköping–Tidaholm: **1.262**
- Höglandet: **1.206**
- Nerikes / Askersund: **1.646**
- Skara–Götene: **1.059**
- Värend: **1.559**

This does not constitute five independent causal estimates because the common counterfactual structure matters, but it shows that the cohort-level result is not generated by a single treated event.

## 8. Severity-threshold pattern

| Outcome | IRR | Pearson-scaled interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beyond start object | **1.953** | **1.217–3.135** |
| Beyond one room | 1.395 | 0.836–2.328 |
| Multiple fire cells | 0.530 | 0.197–1.426 |
| All building-fire records | 0.983 | 0.840–1.150 |

The evidence therefore does **not** support the broad interpretation that full authority pooling caused more severe fires. The strongest signal is concentrated at the first spread threshold measured **at rescue-service arrival**.

One plausible class of mechanisms concerns response geography, mobilisation, coverage, dispatch, transition effects, or incident composition rather than deterioration in final suppression capability. The present design does not identify which mechanism is responsible.

## 9. Legal-form descriptives

- common boards: **IRR 1.714**
- municipal federations: **IRR 2.059**

Both point in the adverse direction. These estimates are exploratory only. With two common-board events and three federation events, no legal-form subgroup inference is warranted.

## 10. Identification limitations

The result remains observational. Important limitations include only three clean pretreatment years; imperfect pretreatment comparability; non-random treatment assignment; heterogeneous pre-A institutional histories; voluntary historical incident-report submission; only two strict post years; possible unmeasured changes in response organisation, geography, staffing or incident mix; an outcome measured at arrival rather than final fire extent; and inability to identify the operational mechanism.

The result should therefore not be interpreted as proof that common boards, federations, consolidation, or pooled authority generally worsen rescue-service outcomes.

## 11. Frozen evidentiary judgment

- **Internal directional robustness:** High.
- **Causal identification:** Moderate–low.
- **Generalisability:** Low.
- **Mechanism specificity:** Low.

## 12. Frozen finding

> **The Historical 2002 Cohort provides a strong and internally robust adverse directional signal at the preregistered primary arrival-spread threshold. Following first verified full authority pooling in 2002, treated rescue-service systems experienced a substantially larger relative increase in the population rate of building fires already spread beyond the start object at rescue-service arrival than the future-treatment control systems. The primary IRR is 1.953, and its direction survives alternative pre-windows, adversarial item-nonresponse assignments, the post-unmask structural-reporting-loss amendment, and removal of every individual treated or control event.**
>
> **The result does not generalise cleanly to higher severity thresholds: the estimate attenuates beyond one room and reverses numerically for multi-firecell spread with high uncertainty. The cohort therefore does not establish that pooled authority generally worsens fire severity or suppression. Given short pretrend support, observational institutional selection and heterogeneous pre-A histories, the result is classified as a robust cohort-level adverse signal and hypothesis-strengthening causal evidence, not confirmatory evidence of a universal treatment effect.**

### Overall evidence label

**ROBUST ADVERSE COHORT SIGNAL / HYPOTHESIS-STRENGTHENING CAUSAL EVIDENCE — NOT CONFIRMATORY GENERAL CAUSAL EVIDENCE**
