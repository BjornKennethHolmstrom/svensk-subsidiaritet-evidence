# Halmstad pre-2010 K Archival Lock v0.1

**Gate:** `K-HALM`  
**Decision:** **PASS_WITH_PROVENANCE_CAVEAT**  
**Protocol-equivalent PASS:** yes  
**Confidence:** moderate-high, bounded historical  
**Consequence:** Halmstad 2012–2014 may now be opened as the prespecified sensitivity donor. It may not replace Jönköping as primary donor.

## 1. Question

The frozen Uppsala protocol required verification that Halmstad already occupied a meaningful **K-without-A** state before 2010, specifically one involving shared/system-level rescue-service leadership rather than only ad hoc mutual aid.

The threshold is not “identical to the modern RäddSam Halland system”. It is:

> Was Halmstad already embedded in a durable cross-municipal operational leadership architecture sufficiently comparable to Uppsala's pre-2012 K state to serve as a sensitivity donor?

## 2. Evidence

### 2007 — HILL was already an operational leadership centre

A 2007 LTU thesis based on direct contact with Halmstad rescue service says SOS-Halland performed initial dispatch and that **HILL then took over**, from the main Halmstad station, staffed by a leadership operator.

This is contemporary evidence that HILL was not created after 2010.

### 2008 — same operational model remains visible

A 2008 Halmstad firefighter recruitment notice again describes SOS-Halland dispatch followed by HILL taking over, with HILL staffed by a trained leadership operator.

This independently confirms the pre-2010 continuity of HILL.

### Official source trail to a 2008 system-leadership document

The strongest pre-2010 evidence is Länsstyrelsen Halland's official 2019 regional oil-spill plan.

When describing resource prioritization within RäddSam Halland via systemledning, its source note cites:

> `HILL (Hallands integrerade larm och ledning), 2008. Ledningssystem-N, insatsledning, systemledning – HILL – Stab-N`

This is direct official provenance that a HILL/system-leadership doctrine existed in **2008**, even though the original 2008 document itself was not recovered in this audit.

### 2009 — active county cooperation

In August 2009 Halmstad rescue chief Hans Ekberg described the county's rescue services as already working extensively together.

That is weaker than the system-leadership evidence, but consistent with an active K network rather than a merely paper arrangement.

### 2011 — explicit nearby confirmation of the architecture

Halmstad's 2011 municipal handlingsprogram makes the structure explicit:

- RäddSam Halland is cooperation among Laholm, Halmstad, Hylte, Falkenberg and Varberg based on a common cooperation agreement;
- **systemledning** over RäddSam Halland resources is exercised from HILL;
- systemledning allocates resources and sets frames for incidents;
- HILL secures readiness over the full area;
- the document explicitly calls it **gemensam systemledning**.

This is post-2010, but it strongly triangulates the meaning of the 2008 HILL/systemledning source trail.

## 3. Version-history caveat

A 2018 MSB/RIB evaluation says the 2016 *Operativa grunder RäddSam N* replaced an earlier `Ledningssystem-N` **from 2011**.

That creates an archival version ambiguity with the Länsstyrelsen plan's citation to a 2008 HILL document titled `Ledningssystem-N, insatsledning, systemledning – HILL – Stab-N`.

The most plausible interpretation is that the leadership architecture and an earlier document existed pre-2010, while a 2011 version revised or formalized the doctrine. But this audit cannot fully reconstruct that document lineage.

This is why the decision is `PASS_WITH_PROVENANCE_CAVEAT`, not an unqualified archival PASS.

## 4. Comparison with Uppsala K

Uppsala's pre-A K evidence consists of long-running county cooperation and shared leadership duty, with later formal study/commitment toward A.

Halmstad's evidence shows, before 2010:

- a dedicated integrated alarm/leadership centre;
- operational takeover after SOS dispatch;
- an official source trail to a 2008 system-leadership doctrine;
- extensive county rescue-service cooperation.

By 2011, that same architecture is explicitly described as cross-municipal systemledning allocating resources and setting incident frames.

Therefore Halmstad is at least sufficiently similar on the relevant dimension:

> **durable operational leadership integration without pooled legal authority.**

It is not treated as institutionally identical to Uppsala, and it remains a sensitivity donor only.

## 5. Gate decision

\boxed{\textbf{K-HALM: PASS WITH PROVENANCE CAVEAT}}
\]

For protocol purposes this satisfies the activation condition for the Halmstad sensitivity donor.
