# Jönköping A=0 Archival Lock v0.1

**Status:** PASS — HIGH, BOUNDED ARCHIVAL CONFIDENCE  
**Protocol consequence:** Uppsala 2012 primary Batch 1 may now be unmasked under the already-frozen Estimator & Analysis Protocol v0.1.  
**Method changes introduced by this lock:** none.

## 1. Question

The frozen Uppsala protocol required one final activation gate before post-treatment B20 outcomes could be opened:

> Did Jönköping remain outside full formal pooled rescue-service authority (A=0), with no identified commitment to enter A, through the end of 2014?

The relevant distinction is:

- **A:** legal/formal pooling of rescue-service authority through a common board, municipal federation or equivalent pooled authority;
- **K:** operational/leadership/resource cooperation while municipal responsibility and organization remain separate.

The gate is not satisfied merely by current institutional status. It requires contemporary evidence around 2012–2014.

## 2. Positive contemporaneous evidence

### 2.1 Municipal governance in 2012

Jönköpings kommun's 2012 annual reporting identifies Stadsbyggnadsnämnden as the board responsible under the Civil Protection Act for rescue-service functions and reports Räddningstjänst as a municipal activity with its own budget and management.

This supports **A=0** in 2012.

### 2.2 Jönköping rescue-service annual report for 2014

The 2014 rescue-service annual report is issued as **Jönköpings kommun — Räddningstjänsten**. It describes:

- municipal goals adopted by kommunfullmäktige and stadsbyggnadsnämnden;
- Räddningstjänsten as a municipal administration/organization;
- eight rescue crews and nine stations within the municipality;
- Jönköping municipal personnel and responsibilities;
- intermunicipal cooperation with Jönköping County municipalities and Ydre;
- a new cooperation agreement signed during 2014.

The report therefore shows the exact coexistence required for a K-without-A donor:

> **municipal organizational responsibility + extensive cross-municipal operational cooperation.**

### 2.3 RäddSam F agreement, September/October 2014

The 2014 *Överenskommelse om samverkan inom RäddSam F* is particularly probative.

It states that municipal rescue-service organizations have cooperated through **samverkansavtal/överenskommelser** for many years. The design is explicitly framed as obtaining the advantages of a larger organization while retaining strong local anchoring in the municipalities.

The agreement is dated 2014-09-01, signed in September/October 2014, and runs through 2018-12-31 unless terminated.

This is strong affirmative evidence that, in late 2014, the chosen institutional form remained **cooperation by agreement**, not transfer of authority to a new common legal rescue-service body.

### 2.4 End-of-2014 municipal budget continuity

Jönköpings kommun Stadsbyggnadsnämnden's 22 January 2015 minutes approve the **internal budget for Räddningstjänsten**. The budget and service memorandum are dated **19 December 2014**.

The decision describes the rescue-service budget as internal economy and operational governance within the municipal administration.

This brackets the end of the protocol's control window and strongly supports that Jönköping remained a municipal rescue-service organization at 2014 year-end.

### 2.5 December 2014 RäddSam F self-description

Räddningsplankan no. 4/2014 describes RäddSam F as a **samarbetsorgan** for 13 municipalities in Jönköping County, Ydre municipality and SOS Alarm.

This independently matches the K classification.

## 3. Targeted negative search for A commitment

A targeted archive/web search was conducted for Jönköping rescue-service material during 2012–2014 using concepts including:

- `kommunalförbund`
- `gemensam nämnd`
- `förbundsbildning`
- transfer/överföring of rescue-service responsibility
- RäddSam F combined with common-board/federation terminology

No relevant rescue-service decision or commitment to create pooled authority was identified.

Search hits for `gemensam nämnd` concerned other municipal service areas. The relevant rescue-service documents instead repeatedly describe the institutional path as municipal responsibility plus agreement-based cooperation.

This is negative evidence and is therefore weaker than an explicit statement saying “no merger is planned”. It is used only in combination with the affirmative contemporary structure evidence above.

## 4. Classification

For the Uppsala 2012 protocol:

- **Jönköping 2012–2014 A-state:** `A=0`
- **Jönköping 2012–2014 K-state:** `K=1`
- **Institutional form:** municipal rescue-service organization participating in extensive agreement-based intermunicipal cooperation
- **Commitment to A through 2014:** none identified in the targeted archival search

## 5. Gate decision

\[
\boxed{\textbf{A0-JKPG: PASS}}
\]

Confidence is classified as **HIGH_BOUNDED_ARCHIVAL**.

The word *bounded* is important. This audit does not claim logical proof that no undiscovered document exists. It concludes that the evidentiary threshold specified before outcome unmasking has been satisfied because:

1. contemporary municipal documents positively show municipal governance/organization;
2. contemporary RäddSam F documents positively identify the cross-municipal form as cooperation by agreement;
3. the September/October 2014 agreement extends that cooperation model beyond the end of 2014;
4. a December 2014 municipal budget record confirms continued municipal governance at year-end;
5. a targeted search found no contradictory A/commitment evidence.
